### `Initial Release`
# **Version 1.0**
Welcome to the first release of Ayumi! Version 1.0 introduces the core Ayumi experience, giving you a private daily photo journal that helps you capture one consistent selfie, build a streak, and watch your face change over time.

This is just the beginning. Future releases will bring further improvements, refinements, and new features based on your feedback.
